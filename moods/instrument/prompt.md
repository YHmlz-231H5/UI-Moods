# Mood: instrument

Instrument is a cold, dense, precision-first interface built on monospace
rhythm, hairline borders and numbered readouts. It feels like lab equipment —
quiet, exact and trustworthy. Every control reads like a measurement, with
tick marks and tabular numbers instead of decoration.

## Keywords

`precision`, `monospace`, `hairline`, `readout`, `dark`

## When to use

- Tool-type interfaces, dashboards and data-monitoring screens
- Anywhere users need to trust the numbers at a glance

## When to avoid

- Casual consumer pages or anything that should feel warm and friendly
