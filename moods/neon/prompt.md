# Mood: neon

Neon is a dark interface lit from behind by signage. Near-black panels hold
faintly translucent glass, and a hot magenta accent throws a wide bloom onto
everything it touches, while a cold cyan-mint carries the success states. Borders
are hairline white at low opacity, so surfaces read as sheets of smoked acrylic
lit from the far side rather than as solid cards. Type is wide and geometric —
the lettering of a late-night shopfront — with an oversized display cut that
floats over the glow. Motion is slow and elastic, easing out with a long,
weighted curve so light appears to bleed and settle rather than blink. It feels
humid, electric and nocturnal: a city street after midnight, still humming.

## Keywords

`neon`, `cyberpunk`, `glow`, `magenta-cyan`

## When to use

- Nightlife, music, gaming and event products that want a nocturnal, electric mood
- Dark showcase screens where a single accent needs to carry the whole hierarchy

## When to avoid

- Long-form reading, docs or any screen that must stay legible in daylight
- Serious enterprise or financial products where neon reads as unserious
