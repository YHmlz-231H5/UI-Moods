# Mood: clay

Clay is warm, soft and matte, like molded ceramics on a sunlit shelf. Organic
shapes, pill buttons, thick gentle shadows with a bright inner highlight, and
a tactile earthy palette. It feels handcrafted, calm and approachable.

## Keywords

`clay`, `matte`, `ceramic`, `organic`, `soft`

## When to use

- Lifestyle and product UIs that should feel warm and approachable
- Editorial or showcase pages with a handcrafted personality

## When to avoid

- Dense data dashboards that need maximum contrast and compact rhythm
