# Mood: sakura

Sakura is a quiet spring afternoon under blossoming branches: a blush-white
ground, panels that hold a soft rose tint, and a deeper petal-pink accent
used like the one saturated note in a pastel painting. Corners are wide and
round, buttons are pills, and a gentle blur behind the cards lets light
through them like petals seen out of focus. Shadows are tinted pink rather
than grey, so nothing ever looks heavy, and the motion settles slowly with
an ease-out, as if drifting. The overall feeling is tender and unhurried —
soft, seasonal and graceful rather than sugary.

## Keywords

`sakura`, `japanese`, `petal`, `gentle`

## When to use

- Lifestyle, beauty, wellness and journaling products with a soft tone
- Cultural, seasonal or gift-oriented experiences meant to feel delicate

## When to avoid

- Serious or high-stakes domains such as finance, legal or medical triage
- Dense, utilitarian tools where low-contrast blush tones reduce clarity
