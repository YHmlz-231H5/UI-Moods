# Mood: eco

Eco is a sunlit greenhouse and leaf litter: a pale botanical ground, clean
white surfaces, and cards that carry a faint blurred translucency so the
foliage behind them seems to bleed through. The accent is a deep forest
green, and buttons are pill-shaped rather than squared, giving every action
a soft, organic profile. Radii are generous and the shadows are tinted green
rather than grey, so elevation reads as dappled light rather than as
paperweight. Motion is slower and eases out gently, like leaves settling.
The overall feeling is fresh and unhurried — sustainable, natural and
deliberately gentle rather than preachy.

## Keywords

`sustainability`, `organic`, `green`, `calm`

## When to use

- Sustainability reports, eco-brand storefronts and carbon-tracking dashboards
- Wellness, outdoor and slow-living products that want a natural, warm register

## When to avoid

- Financial or compliance tooling, where the softness undermines precision
- Interfaces needing maximum density or a stark, high-contrast treatment
