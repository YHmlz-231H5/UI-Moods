# Mood: anime

Anime is high-saturation kawaii energy: rounded bold type, sticker buttons
with thick outlines and hard shadows, candy gradients and sparkles. Every
control is drawn with personality — cheerful, playful and unmistakably fun.

## Keywords

`kawaii`, `sticker`, `halftone`, `sparkle`

## When to use

- Games, fan products and youth-facing apps
- Anything that should feel energetic and playful

## When to avoid

- Serious, corporate or data-dense tools
