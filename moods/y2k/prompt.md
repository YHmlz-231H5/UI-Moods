# Mood: y2k

Y2k is early-millennium optimism rendered as chrome and bubble. A pale
ice-blue ground holds white glossy surfaces with an airbrushed gradient, and
corners swell to fully rounded pills — buttons, badges and inputs all end in
soft capsules rather than edges. A saturated violet accent glows through
translucent panels, and the shadow beneath each one is coloured light rather
than grey, so the whole page looks lit from within. Type is Verdana with
generous, friendly proportions. Motion is the star: a springy overshoot curve
that lets every control bounce past its target and settle back, the way
early-2000s web toys did. It feels glossy, giddy and slightly nostalgic — a
site from a better-optimistic decade.

## Keywords

`y2k`, `chrome`, `bubble`, `gradient`

## When to use

- Consumer launches, creator tools and nostalgia-driven brand campaigns
- Music, fashion and streetwear pages aimed at a Gen Z or millennial audience

## When to avoid

- Formal or high-stakes products where bouncing motion undermines credibility
- Data-dense tools where glossy gradients compete with the numbers
