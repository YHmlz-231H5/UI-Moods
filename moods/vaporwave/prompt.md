# Mood: vaporwave

Vaporwave is a sunset replayed from a CRT. The field is deep violet, lit
from behind by magenta and cyan, and the panels are not surfaces but sheets
of tinted glass — six percent white over purple, ten percent pink — floating
on a ten-pixel blur that smears the glow behind them into soft horizontal
bands. Magenta is the loud colour: it owns the primary button and leaks a
twenty-six-pixel halo into the surrounding dark, while a second voice of
electric cyan and a third of acid yellow answer it as status colours. Type
is VT323, a pixel face that glows like a phosphor tube, paired with Courier
New for the moments the pixels must behave. Edges are softened but not
tamed — eight-pixel blocks, twelve-pixel cards — and the single pill badge
is the softest touch of all. Motion runs long and decelerating on a wide
cubic curve, so panels drift in like a memory surfacing. The mood feels
humid, nostalgic and synthetically warm.

## Keywords

`vaporwave`, `synthwave`, `magenta-cyan`, `retro-future`

## When to use

- Music, mixtape, arcade and retro-game showcases
- Nostalgic landing pages, digital-art portfolios and moody side projects

## When to avoid

- Corporate, clinical or financial interfaces needing sober neutrality
- Long-form reading where phosphor glow reduces legibility
