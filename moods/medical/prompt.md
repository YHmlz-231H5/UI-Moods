# Mood: medical

Medical is a calm, clinical surface drawn from hospital wayfinding and
sterile supply rooms: a faintly teal-tinted ground, immaculate white panels
and soft, rounded corners that take the sharpness out of the geometry. The
accent is a deep medical teal — reassuring and hygienic rather than alarming
— set against text in a desaturated deep teal-grey that never strains the
eye. Source Sans 3 gives labels an open, humane rhythm, and shadows fall
softly and widely, like light diffused through a frosted window. The overall
feeling is quiet competence: an interface that reassures you while it tells
you something serious.

## Keywords

`healthcare`, `clinical`, `calm`, `teal`

## When to use

- Patient portals, appointment booking and health-tracking applications
- Clinical tooling where a calm, non-alarming atmosphere reassures the user

## When to avoid

- High-energy consumer apps that need excitement or urgency
- Situations requiring bold, attention-grabbing alerts
