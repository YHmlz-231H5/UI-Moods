# Mood: food

Food is a warm table under soft daylight. The canvas is a cream-white blush
of #fff8f2 — deliberately never pure, so colour reads as served rather than
sterile — and every panel is a clean sheet of white lifted by the faintest
orange shadow, as if it were resting on a paper menu. Corners are generous
and forgiving: cards bow to sixteen pixels, buttons stretch into full pills,
and the whole layout feels as though it could be picked up with one hand.
Type is geometric and round in Poppins and Nunito, set large enough to be
read at arm's length across a counter. Colour arrives in a single warm
accent, a burnt orange that stains the shadows and the buttons alike, while
green, amber and red are reserved strictly for whether the dish is fresh,
close to order, or ruined. Motion is slow and eased, a server setting a
plate down rather than a machine switching. The mood feels open,
hospitable and mouth-watering.

## Keywords

`food`, `appetizing`, `warm`, `menu`

## When to use

- Restaurant menus, recipe cards and delivery ordering screens
- Café storefronts, food blogs and packaged-goods showcases

## When to avoid

- Dense dashboards where soft shadows and pills blur the hierarchy
- Dark, high-tension tools that need a harder visual register
