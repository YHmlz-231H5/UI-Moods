# Mood: brutal

Brutal treats the screen like a screen-printed poster stapled to concrete. Flat
panels sit on a cold grey ground, every edge cut square, every divider a heavy
black rule. Depth comes from solid offset shadows — a black slab dropped hard
down and to the right, never blurred — and typography shouts in a black-weight
grotesque with oversized display lines and tight uppercase labels. A single
saturated yellow acts as the ink that got pressed slightly out of register.
Motion is abrupt and mechanical: state changes snap in two steps like a
physical switch, with no easing, no slide, no softness anywhere. It feels
loud, physical and confrontational — a poster you can read from across the
room.

## Keywords

`brutalist`, `hard-shadow`, `zero-radius`, `high-contrast`

## When to use

- Campaign pages, launch microsites and editorial posters where shouting is the goal
- Portfolio and agency work that wants raw, unpolished confidence

## When to avoid

- Dense dashboards or data tables that need calm reading and soft grouping
- Banking, healthcare or enterprise tools where hard edges read as hostile
