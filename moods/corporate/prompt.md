# Mood: corporate

Corporate is a cool, orderly workspace in a well-lit office: cool grey-blue
canvas, clean white panels and hairline borders that separate information
without ever shouting. The accent is a single confident blue, used sparingly
on the one action that matters, while typography stays neutral and dense —
Inter at a tight body size, tuned for tables, forms and long reading
sessions. Corners are modest and consistent, shadows are shallow enough to
feel like paper rather than plastic, and motion is quick and unfussy. The
overall feeling is composed and accountable: the interface of a system that
expects to be trusted with real work.

## Keywords

`saas`, `enterprise`, `data-dense`, `trustworthy`

## When to use

- Enterprise dashboards, admin consoles and internal tools that carry dense data
- B2B products where credibility and legibility matter more than atmosphere

## When to avoid

- Consumer or lifestyle products that need warmth and personality
- Long-form editorial reading, where the density would tire the eye
