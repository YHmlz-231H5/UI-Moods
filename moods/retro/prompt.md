# Mood: retro

Retro is a 1970s printed catalogue: warm cream paper, chunky 2px rules and
hard offset shadows that stamp every card flat onto the page with no
blur at all. Type is Georgia — a bookish serif with generous counters — and
the palette stays in burnt orange, ochre and deep brown, the way cheap
spot colour behaved on uncoated stock. Corners are small and slightly
square, spacing is roomy, and the display size is large enough to read as a
headline in a catalogue rather than a heading in an app. The overall feeling
is tactile and print-born — warm, deliberate and a little proud of its
roughness.

## Keywords

`seventies`, `paper`, `warm`, `chunky`

## When to use

- Brand and campaign sites that want a printed, analogue nostalgia
- Products with a handmade, heritage or retro-kitsch story to tell

## When to avoid

- Data-heavy tools and dashboards, where hard shadows and heavy rules tire the eye
- Products that must read as modern, minimal or high-performance
