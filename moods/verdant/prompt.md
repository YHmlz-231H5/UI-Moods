# Mood: verdant

Verdant is a phosphor-green terminal glow — monochrome luminous readouts,
scanline energy and a retro-futuristic tool feel. It is single-voice,
monospace and unmistakably alive.

## Keywords

`phosphor`, `crt`, `terminal`, `mono`, `green`

## When to use

- Terminal-style tools, monitors and telemetry
- Geek-culture products that should feel nostalgic and technical

## When to avoid

- Warm consumer interfaces or anything needing a soft, rounded feel
