# Mood: bauhaus

Bauhaus builds the page from primitives. A warm oat ground carries flat fields
of signal red, deep teal and mustard, laid on like cut paper rather than
rendered as depth — circles, squares and hard diagonal blocks that never round
or soften. Type is a geometric sans, all circles and open forms, set small and
proudly. Every rule is a solid black line, and the only depth cue is a short
offset shadow block that reads as a stacked sheet of card. Motion is quick and
mechanical, a hard stop curve with nothing elastic about it. The result feels
handmade, constructive and civic — a workshop notice board where every shape
was placed on purpose.

## Keywords

`bauhaus`, `primary-colors`, `geometric`, `constructivist`

## When to use

- Cultural institutions, museums and archive sites with a modernist voice
- Brand and marketing pages built from strong primary-colour blocks

## When to avoid

- Products needing long-form comfort reading or subtle data hierarchy
- Interfaces where brand guidelines already lock the palette
