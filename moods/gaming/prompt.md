# Mood: gaming

Gaming is a black cockpit cut from hard-edged metal. Panels sit flat and
matte on a near-black field, their edges chamfered into sharp four-pixel
corners, and every border is a hairline no thicker than a circuit trace.
Light does not fill the room — it leaks out of the interface: electric cyan
pours off the primary buttons in a tight twenty-pixel halo, while status
green, warning amber and hot pink flash like a telemetry readout. Type is
condensed and technical, Rajdhani and Chakra Petch squeezed into narrow
ladders of uppercase numerals and short labels. Nothing drifts; every state
change snaps through in three discrete steps, like a boot sequence, as if the
machine is deciding rather than animating. The whole thing feels
competitive, technical and slightly dangerous — built for lobbies, matches
and live leaderboards, not for reading.

## Keywords

`gaming`, `hud`, `angular`, `cyan-on-black`

## When to use

- Esports dashboards, match lobbies and live score overlays
- Game launchers, tournament brackets and player-stat screens

## When to avoid

- Reading-heavy editorial content that needs long-form calm
- Consumer shopping flows where hard corners and glow read as hostile
