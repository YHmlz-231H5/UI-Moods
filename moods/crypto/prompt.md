# Mood: crypto

Crypto is a terminal room in the dark. The ground is a near-black charcoal,
the panels a shade lighter, the surfaces a shade lighter still — three flat
planes stacked with no gloss, no blur and almost no shadow, like matte
instrument housings. A single acid-lime accent carries every action and
every live number, throwing a restrained green glow so that prices read as
values on a ticker rather than as buttons. Everything is set in IBM Plex
Mono or JetBrains Mono: monospaced columns, hard alignment, characters that
step forward in a steady 150-millisecond beat on a linear easing with no
spring, because markets do not bounce. Corners are tight and utilitarian at
four to six pixels, and the only soft shape in the system is the pill badge.
It feels clinical, obsessive and always-on — the interface of someone
watching a chart at three in the morning.

## Keywords

`crypto`, `terminal`, `lime`, `monospace`

## When to use

- Trading terminals, wallets, balances and transaction history
- Developer tooling for blockchain, chains and protocol explorers

## When to avoid

- Consumer or lifestyle sites where a terminal register reads as cold
- Marketing pages that need warmth, photography and visual softness
