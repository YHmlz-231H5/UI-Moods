# Mood: nordic

Nordic is a bright, undecorated room in a Copenhagen apartment: a near-white
warm ground, barely-there borders and a muted green accent used so sparingly
it feels like a single plant on the sill. Almost nothing is rounded — radii
stay tight and square, shadows are barely present, and the whole layout
relies on generous spacing rather than outlines to create structure. Type is
set in Inter with a quiet hierarchy, and the composition is allowed to breathe
against a pale, low-chroma surface. The overall feeling is calm and
functional: beauty arrived at by subtraction, with nothing there that
cannot justify itself.

## Keywords

`scandinavian`, `muted`, `functional`, `airy`

## When to use

- Minimal product sites and portfolio pages that want space to breathe
- Utilities and settings screens where clarity and low friction are the goal

## When to avoid

- Expressive or playful brands that need colour and personality
- Dense, information-heavy applications that would feel under-designed
