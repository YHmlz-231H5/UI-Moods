# Mood: forest

Forest is a soft naturalist palette — moss green, wood and linen, organic
rounded shapes. It feels like a calm botanical notebook: quiet, grounded and
handcrafted.

## Keywords

`moss`, `wood`, `botanical`, `organic`

## When to use

- Nature, lifestyle and wellness products
- Content that should feel calm, warm and organic

## When to avoid

- Cold technical dashboards or high-density data tools
