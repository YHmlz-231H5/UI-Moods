# Mood: travel

Travel is an open window onto clear daylight. The background is a pale
sky-blue wash, the panels are white as boarding-pass paper, and thin
blue-grey borders sketch in the layout like shorelines on a map. Nothing
casts a heavy shadow — depth comes instead from a soft, wide, translucent
blur that lets the blue underneath drift through the cards. Corners stay
rounded and approachable at ten to sixteen pixels, and the accent is the
deep teal-blue of an ocean chart, used sparingly on primary actions so that
bookings, filters and prices carry the eye. Type is a confident geometric
sans in Montserrat and Inter, wide and even, like signage at an airport.
Motion eases out over a quarter of a second and never springs, so the
interface glides rather than bounces. The mood feels calm, spacious and
expectant — the visual equivalent of checking in early with a window seat.

## Keywords

`travel`, `booking`, `airy`, `sky`

## When to use

- Flight, hotel and itinerary booking journeys
- Destination guides, trip planners and travel deal pages

## When to avoid

- Dark or nocturnal themes where a pale sky would feel wrong
- Dense, data-heavy tools that need tighter spacing and stronger contrast
