# Mood: monochrome

Monochrome removes colour so only structure remains. Every surface is a step
of grey on white — near-white panels, faintly warmer greys for cards — and
even the status colours collapse into values, so warnings and errors are
told apart by weight and by shape rather than by hue. Pure black is the only
strong statement: it carries the primary action and the boldest type. Corners
are barely rounded, borders are a single hairline, and there are no shadows,
no blur and no glow. Type is Inter at a neutral, unsentimental scale, and
motion is a plain short ease that never draws attention to itself. It feels
clinical, restrained and deliberate — the interface stripped to its skeleton
so the content is the only thing left to look at.

## Keywords

`monochrome`, `greyscale`, `high-contrast`, `minimal`

## When to use

- Data tools, admin panels and developer-facing interfaces where clarity wins
- Accessibility-first or print-preview contexts where colour carries no meaning

## When to avoid

- Products that rely on colour-coded status to be understood at a glance
- Marketing pages that need emotional personality from the palette
