# Mood: aurora

Aurora is cold night sky lit from within. The base is a deep blue-black, the
colour of water at midnight, and the panels above it are barely-there sheets
of five-percent white while the surfaces carry a faint ten-percent ice-blue.
The light in this interface does not come from the panels, it passes through
them: a fourteen-pixel blur smears the background into slow, drifting bands.
The accent is a mint-teal as fresh as glacier melt, breathing outward in a
wide thirty-pixel halo around buttons and focus rings, while green, amber
and rose sit underneath it as quiet status signals. Type is Outfit and
Inter, geometric and open, set on generous twelve-to-eighteen-pixel curves
so that nothing feels machined. Blocks, cards and inputs are the softest in
the system, and the badge is a full pill. Motion is slow and settled, a
four-tenths curve that eases toward rest rather than snapping into it. The
mood feels vast, cold and quietly alive — standing under a sky that is still
moving.

## Keywords

`aurora`, `gradient`, `teal-violet`, `deep`

## When to use

- Immersive landing pages, music players and media-rich showcases
- Ambient dashboards, wellness apps and deep-focus creative tools

## When to avoid

- Data-dense consoles where translucent panels blur the information
- Warm, editorial or food-led brands that need human warmth
