# Mood: finance

Finance is a private bank at night: an inky, near-black ground with panels
raised just enough to be distinguished by a hairline edge, and a single
antique-gold accent that behaves like a hallmark stamped onto a vault door.
Type is IBM Plex Sans with a heavy display size for figures, and numerals are
expected to be read in columns, so alignment does the work that decoration
might do elsewhere. Danger is outlined rather than filled, which keeps the
destructive action present without letting it dominate the screen. The
overall feeling is discreet, expensive and unhurried — money treated as
something serious and worth keeping behind glass.

## Keywords

`banking`, `noir`, `gold`, `tabular-numbers`

## When to use

- Banking, trading and investment products where trust and value are the message
- Financial dashboards read in low light, or on large desk-side displays

## When to avoid

- Children's products, playful campaigns and informal community spaces
- Interfaces that need to feel warm, open and immediately approachable
