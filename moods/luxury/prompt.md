# Mood: luxury

Luxury is quiet, editorial and precise — near-black ink on warm paper,
hairline rules, generous whitespace and a serif display. It feels like a
high-end print magazine: restrained, expensive and confident.

## Keywords

`luxury`, `editorial`, `serif`, `hairline`, `minimal`

## When to use

- Brand sites, lookbooks and premium product pages
- Content that should feel calm, spacious and expensive

## When to avoid

- Dense tool UIs or anything needing high-control density
