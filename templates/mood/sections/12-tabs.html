How is revenue calculated?Semantic layer · updated 4 Mar 2026
−

Revenue is the sum of settled invoices minus refunds and credits, converted to the workspace currency at the daily reference rate.

Only invoices in the settled state are counted, so pending payments never inflate a dashboard.
