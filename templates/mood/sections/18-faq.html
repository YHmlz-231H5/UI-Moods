FAQ
How does Beacon bill for event volume?

We count billable events after sampling, so server-side heartbeats and debug traffic never reach your invoice. Your dashboard shows the running count for the current cycle and the exact rate applied on the next invoice.

Can we keep raw events in our own cloud account?

Yes. The collector writes to your own bucket first, and Beacon only stores the aggregated rows it queries. Point the collector at an S3, GCS or Azure target and switch on the warehouse-sync flag in your workspace settings.

What happens when we cross the event quota?

We never drop data and we never surprise you with a bill. At 80% of quota the workspace owner gets an email, at 100% collection continues in low-detail mode and dashboards keep working on the aggregates we already have.

Do you offer a free trial for Enterprise?

Enterprise pilots run for 30 days with the full feature set and a named engineer attached. There is no card on file and no automatic conversion at the end of the pilot.

Can we self-host the Beacon collector?

The collector ships as a signed container and a static binary, both published with checksums. It runs on your edge fleet with no outbound dependency beyond the ingest endpoint.

How do you handle GDPR deletion requests?

Beacon stores pseudonymous identifiers only. A deletion request removes every row for that subject within 24 hours across primary storage, backups and warehouse exports, and the workspace keeps an audit record of the request.
