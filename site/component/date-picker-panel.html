Date Picker Panel 日期选择面板

The month panel a date picker drops: the month and its navigation, the week grid, and the two actions in the footer. The panel does not keep a month, so the host owns the month and the buttons that change it.

div form since 1.1.0

The panel does not keep a month. A month panel cannot know which months exist, so the host owns the month, the host owns the buttons that change it, and the host owns the words in the footer. What the panel gives the host is a grid it can drop into any frame, drawn the way the calendar draws it: today is a filled circle with a number on it, so it is a surface with text and takes the brand's on-colour; selected is a ring with nothing on it, so it is a shape and takes the graphic form of the brand colour; disabled is a number that takes the derived disabled colour, and it is marked with aria-disabled rather than with a class of its own. A selected day that stands inside a range stands on the brand's tint and not on the panel's surface, so there it takes the foreground the contract writes for that tint, and its ring takes the brand colour as text — the pair the contract writes for a mark on a soft surface.

Props

NameTypeDefaultDescription
monthstring2026-11The month to draw, written as YYYY-MM. The panel has no navigation of its own, so the host decides which month this is.
weekdaysstringMon,Tue,Wed,Thu,Fri,Sat,SunThe seven column names, separated by commas. They are written out rather than derived, so a month can start on Monday or on Sunday, and so a host can name the columns in its own language.
todaystring2026-11-28The date that is today, written as YYYY-MM-DD. The day it names takes the today marker.
selectedstring2026-11-20The date the reader has picked, written as YYYY-MM-DD. The day it names takes the selected marker.
model-valuestring—The bound date. It answers to `v-model` as well as to `selected`, and the binding wins when it is there.
disabledstring2026-11-05,2026-11-06The dates that cannot be picked, written as YYYY-MM-DD and separated by commas. The days they name are marked with aria-disabled, so the reader is told they cannot be picked.
show-footerbooleantrueWhether the footer is shown. Without it the panel has no footer, and the host keeps the two actions somewhere else.
footer-textstring确定The words on the confirm button.
show-confirmbooleantrueWhether the confirm button is shown. Without it the footer keeps only the shortcut, and the host keeps the action somewhere else.
typeenum = single | rangesingleWhether the panel holds one month or two. The single panel is the base class on its own; the range panel puts two months side by side and adds um-date-picker-panel--range.
borderbooleantrueWhether the panel draws a frame of its own. Turn it off and the border goes and the host draws the frame, or the panel sits inside one. It adds um-date-picker-panel--borderless.
single-panelbooleanfalseWhether a range is picked one month at a time. It adds um-date-picker-panel--single-panel, and the second month is not drawn.
clearablebooleanfalseWhether the footer carries a clear action. It adds um-date-picker-panel--clearable. The panel keeps no value, so what a press on it means is the host's business, the way it is for the other two actions.
show-week-numberbooleanfalseWhether each week says which week of the year it is. It adds um-date-picker-panel--week-number, and the numbers are a column of their own on the start side of the grid.

Slots

NameDescription
defaultWhat goes in a day of the panel. It is called once per day with that day's date, and what it fills sits beside the number, so a day the host filled is still marked as today or as selected.
prev-monthThe mark in the button that goes back a month. The drawn chevron steps aside for it, so the host's own mark is what the reader sees.
next-monthThe mark in the button that goes forward a month. The drawn chevron steps aside for it, so the host's own mark is what the reader sees.

Events

NameDescription
clickFires when one of the buttons is pressed. It is the browser's own event — the library installs no listener, and the panel does not keep a month, so what a press means is the host's business.

Accessibility

Role: group

Keyboard: Tab

The month is a grid: the weekday names are a row of column headers and the weeks are rows of grid cells, so the browser says which column a day belongs to and a reader can walk the week across. A day that cannot be picked carries aria-disabled, so the reader is told that day is unavailable; the other markers are classes and not attributes, so the reader is not told which day is today or which one is selected: the host has to say that in the markup or in the text around the grid. The two buttons in the header and the two in the footer are real buttons, so the browser announces them and the keyboard can reach them — but the panel does not keep a month, so what a press means is the host's business.
