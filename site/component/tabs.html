Tabs 标签页

One row of tabs over several panels of content. The tabs are radio inputs, so the browser keeps the selection, the focus and the arrow keys without a line of script.

input, label, section navigation since 1.1.0

The tabs are radio inputs, and each radio sits inside the label of its own tab, so the browser sees one group of radios and holds it as a single stop in the tab order: Tab lands on the group, the arrow keys move along it, and the browser keeps exactly one radio picked. The stylesheet pairs the Nth radio with the Nth panel by position, so the panels are <section>s after the bar of tabs — `nth-of-type` has to count them apart from the <div> that holds the header. Four panels are wired out of the box; a fifth needs one more selector in this file, not a line of JavaScript. There is no add mark, no close mark and no rename field: adding a tab means writing a new radio and a new panel, closing one means taking it out of the document, and renaming one means editing a word, and none of those is something a stylesheet can do. A host that wants them brings its own controls.

Props

NameTypeDefaultDescription
namestring—The name the radios share. Two tab groups with different names can be open at the same time.
typeenum = line | cardlineThe shape of the tab row. The line type is the base class on its own; the card type adds um-tabs--card.
selectednumber1Which tab starts open. It is the checked attribute on that radio.
model-valuenumber—Which tab is open, counted from one. The binding wins over `selected`.
stretchbooleanfalseWhether the tabs fill the width they are given. It adds um-tabs--stretch.
tab-positionenum = top | bottomtopWhere the bar of tabs sits. The bar on top is the base class on its own; the bar under the panel adds um-tabs--bottom.
disabledbooleanfalseWhether the tabs can be picked. The radios carry the real disabled attribute, so the browser refuses to select them and the tab leaves the tab order; the class paints the disabled treatment. A single tab is disabled by putting the same attribute on its own radio in the markup.

Slots

NameDescription
panelThe content of one tab. It goes inside the panel section.
defaultThe labels of the tabs, one child per tab.
labelThe label of one tab. It is called once per tab with its index, the way the panel slot is.

Events

NameDescription
changeFires when another tab is picked. It is the radio group's own change event — the library installs no listener.

Accessibility

Role: radiogroup

Keyboard: Tab Arrow keys Space

The header is a radio group and the control under each tab is a radio input. The radio is the only thing in the markup that can carry the selection, and it carries it natively: the browser announces the picked tab as checked and the reader knows which one is open. That is also why the header says radiogroup and not tablist — a tablist promises tabs, and a tab has to say aria-selected, which cannot be written without JavaScript and would be stale the moment the reader picked another tab. The panels say group for the same reason: there is no tab to own a tab panel. What the reader gets is a group of radios with one of them checked, which is exactly what the widget is.
