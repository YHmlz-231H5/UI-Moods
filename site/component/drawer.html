Drawer 抽屉

A panel that slides out from the edge of the page: a side panel with a title, a body and a row of actions. It is held against the edge with fixed positioning, so it stays put while the page moves behind it.

aside, h2, div, button feedback since 1.1.0

The panel paints one surface and nothing inside it paints another: a header, a body and a footer are separated by rules rather than by fills, so the surface the reader sees is the surface the contract describes. There is no mask behind the panel, for the same reason the dialog has none: the contract has no role for a scrim, and a second translucent layer over the page is a colour no role names. What stands between the panel and the page is the shadow and the rule on its free edge.

Examples

Basic · 基础用法
A panel on the right edge of the page, with a title, a body and a row of actions. It is fixed to the viewport, so the page moves behind it rather than under it.

Filters

Only the builds that failed in the last hour.

<aside class="um-drawer" aria-labelledby="um-drawer-title">
  <div class="um-drawer__header">
    <h2 class="um-drawer__title" id="um-drawer-title">Filters</h2>
    <button class="um-drawer__close" type="button" aria-label="Close"></button>
  </div>
  <div class="um-drawer__body">
    <p>Only the builds that failed in the last hour.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="um-drawer__footer">
    <button type="button">Reset</button>
    <button type="button">Apply</button>
  </div>
</aside>
Directions · 方向
The panel can come from any of the four edges. They are shown here in the layout rather than over the page, so that all three can be compared side by side.

From the left

The rule moves to the right side, because that is the edge the panel hangs from.

From the top

A panel that comes from the top measures down instead of across.

From the bottom

And one that comes from the bottom measures down as well.

Props

NameTypeDefaultDescription
directionenum = right | left | top | bottomrightWhich edge the panel slides out from. The right edge is the base class on its own; the other three add um-drawer--left, um-drawer--top or um-drawer--bottom.
sizeenum = sm | md | lgmdHow wide the panel is. A panel that comes from the side measures across; one that comes from the top or the bottom measures down. The size is a measure of the panel's own type, so it grows with the mood's text.
inlinebooleanfalseWhether the panel is part of the layout instead of floating over it. It adds um-drawer--inline.
titlestring—The heading of the panel.

Slots

NameDescription
titleWhat is inside the heading.
defaultThe body of the panel.
footerThe actions in the footer.

Events

NameDescription
closeFires when the close button is pressed. A drawer is not a dialog, so the browser has no close of its own: the panel stays where it is until the host takes it away.

Accessibility

Role: complementary

Keyboard: Tab Enter

The panel is an <aside>, so it is a complementary landmark, and it names itself with aria-labelledby pointing at its own title — so a page with two drawers tells them apart. The title is a real heading, so the reader can jump to it. The panel is not modal: it does not take the focus, it does not make the page behind it inert, and it does not close itself, because all of that belongs to a dialog. The close button is a real button with a name; what it does is the host's, and the library only gives it a shape.
