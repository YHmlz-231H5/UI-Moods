Card 卡片

A surface that groups one thing: a title, a body, a footer. Its border, radius, shadow and fill all come from the mood, so one card reads correctly in all thirty-two of them.

div data since 1.1.0

The frosted variant is a mood capability, not a card colour: it applies the theme's backdrop filter and changes nothing else. A mood without one declares `none`, so the hook is invisible there.

Examples

A card · 一张卡
Header, body and footer are optional on their own, but the order is fixed: header, then body, then footer. The title is a heading, so the page keeps its outline.

Deployment

Three nodes were updated in the last hour. Nothing is waiting for approval.

Updated 14:20

<div class="um-card">
  <div class="um-card__header">
    <h3 class="um-card__title">Deployment</h3>
  </div>
  <div class="um-card__body">
    <p>Three nodes were updated in the last hour. Nothing is waiting for approval.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="um-card__footer">
    <p>Updated 14:20</p>
  </div>
</div>
Frosted · 磨砂
The frosted variant applies the mood's backdrop filter. A mood that has none declares `none`, so on that theme the class is invisible and the card looks the same as any other.

Glass

On a mood with a backdrop filter, the card blurs what is behind it.

<div class="um-card um-card--frosted">
  <div class="um-card__header">
    <h3 class="um-card__title">Glass</h3>
  </div>
  <div class="um-card__body">
    <p>On a mood with a backdrop filter, the card blurs what is behind it.</p>
  </div>
</div>

Props

variantenum = frosted—Which surface the card has. Without a variant the card is the mood's plain overlay surface.

Accessibility

The card is a plain container and carries no role. Put a real heading in `.um-card__title` — the class sets the size, so the document outline stays right. If the whole card is meant to be clickable, put a link inside it instead of making the card a button: a clickable div is not focusable and is not announced as a control.
