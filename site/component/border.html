Border 边框

A frame of one semantic colour around a block of content. It says what state the content is in without adding a word to it.

div basic since 1.1.0

The frame paints the surface and the body inside it paints nothing. Two translucent layers would be a colour no role names, and a guarantee made against one of them is a guarantee about a surface nobody sees. The frame's own colour is the graphic form of the tone, because the frame is a shape that carries the state on its own: the raw fill colour clears 4.5:1 as a background for text and clears nothing as a picture.

Examples

The five states · 五种状态
Each frame carries the colour of the state it announces. The colour is the graphic form of the tone, because the frame is a shape whose colour says it all — the raw fill colour clears nothing as a picture.

Accessibility

Keyboard: Tab

The frame is a div and adds nothing to the accessibility tree: a border is a shape, and the content inside it is read as it is. When the frame holds a control, the frame takes the focus ring through :focus-within, so a keyboard reader can see where they are. Give the frame a role and an accessible name only when the group of controls inside it needs one.
