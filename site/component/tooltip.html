Tooltip 文字提示

A short note that appears when the reader points at a word or tabs to it. The bubble is drawn by the stylesheet and placed with absolute positioning; the trigger is a real button, so the keyboard opens it as well as the pointer.

button, span feedback since 1.1.0

The arrow is a square turned on its corner, with its two lower edges carrying the bubble's border colour and its fill taken from the bubble's own surface. That is what makes the point read as part of the bubble instead of a shape glued to it — the border line stops at the arrow and picks up on the other side.

Examples

Basic · 基础用法
A button with a note under it. The bubble is written after the trigger and points at it with aria-describedby.
Compares the two ledgers and writes the differences back. 
<span class="um-tooltip">
  <button class="um-tooltip__trigger" type="button" aria-describedby="um-tooltip-note">Reconcile</button>
  <span class="um-tooltip__content" id="um-tooltip-note" role="tooltip">Compares the two ledgers and writes the differences back.<span class="um-tooltip__arrow" aria-hidden="true"></span></span>
</span>
Placements · 位置
The base class puts the bubble under the trigger. The three modifiers move it above, to the left or to the right, and turn the arrow to match.
The bubble hangs above the trigger. The bubble stands to the left. The bubble stands to the right. 
<span class="um-tooltip um-tooltip--top">
  <button class="um-tooltip__trigger" type="button">Above</button>
  <span class="um-tooltip__content" role="tooltip">The bubble hangs above the trigger.<span class="um-tooltip__arrow" aria-hidden="true"></span></span>
</span>
<span class="um-tooltip um-tooltip--left">
  <button class="um-tooltip__trigger" type="button">Left</button>
  <span class="um-tooltip__content" role="tooltip">The bubble stands to the left.<span class="um-tooltip__arrow" aria-hidden="true"></span></span>
</span>
<span class="um-tooltip um-tooltip--right">
  <button class="um-tooltip__trigger" type="button">Right</button>
  <span class="um-tooltip__content" role="tooltip">The bubble stands to the right.<span class="um-tooltip__arrow" aria-hidden="true"></span></span>
</span>
A link as the trigger · 链接做触发器
The trigger does not have to be a button. A link works the same way, and it is announced as a link rather than as a button.
the runbook How a failed run is put back in order, step by step. 
<span class="um-tooltip">
  <a class="um-tooltip__trigger" href="#um-tooltip-doc" aria-describedby="um-tooltip-link-note">the runbook</a>
  <span class="um-tooltip__content" id="um-tooltip-link-note" role="tooltip">How a failed run is put back in order, step by step.<span class="um-tooltip__arrow" aria-hidden="true"></span></span>
</span>

Accessibility

Role: tooltip

Keyboard: Tab Enter Space

The trigger is a real <button> or <a>, so it is in the tab order and the browser announces it; the bubble is a plain span with role="tooltip", and the trigger points at it with aria-describedby so the reader hears the note as a description of the trigger. The bubble opens on focus as well as on hover, so the keyboard reaches everything the pointer reaches. A CSS-only tooltip cannot be dismissed with Escape — the bubble closes when the focus leaves the trigger.
