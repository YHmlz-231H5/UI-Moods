Layout 布局

The skeleton of a page: a header, a side column, the content and a footer. It draws the rules that separate them and leaves the surfaces to the page behind it.

div, header, aside, main, footer basic since 1.1.0

The layout paints no surface of its own. A frame that owns a surface paints it and the thing inside the frame paints nothing, and the layout has no business owning the page's background — the page behind it does. The regions are separated with rules instead, so the skeleton reads as a skeleton and the page keeps one background all the way down.

Accessibility

The parts are the landmarks themselves: a <header>, an <aside>, a <main> and a <footer>. A screen reader can jump from one landmark to the next, which is the fastest way through a long page. There is one <main> per page, so the content of the layout is the main landmark and nothing else may claim it. Label the aside and the header when there is more than one of either.
